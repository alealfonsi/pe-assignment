# Customer Risk Classification Standard

## Risk levels

Analyses produced for operator review classify a customer's recent activity into one of four
levels. The level describes the activity under review, not the customer permanently.

- LOW: activity is consistent with the customer's profile and history; no rule signals, or
  only isolated low-weight signals with a plausible explanation.
- MEDIUM: one or more signal families present but limited in value, frequency or intensity;
  or anomalies explainable by profile changes (new business activity, relocation). Warrants
  documentation and routine follow-up.
- HIGH: sustained or combined signal families (e.g. repeated high-value cross-border
  transfers plus structuring; card testing followed by successful CNP spending), greylist
  corridor concentration, or rapid crypto dispersal. Warrants EDD referral within 5 business
  days and possible feature restrictions.
- CRITICAL: signals with mandatory same-day escalation - mixer exposure, Tier 1
  jurisdiction involvement, active confirmed fraud, or apparent sanctions evasion.

## Grading principles

Grade on the combination of signals, not the count alone: two corroborating families
(structuring + high-risk corridor) outweigh many repetitions of one family. Consider
customer segment context: business accounts legitimately produce larger and more frequent
payments; premium retail customers produce high-value travel and luxury card spending.
Recency matters: signals in the last 14 days weigh more than a quiet historical pattern.
Failed and reversed transactions count: attempted behaviour is behaviour.

## Required analysis content

Every analysis must state: the assigned risk level with the principal driver named; findings
each tied to concrete transactions or patterns from the data (never invented); and
recommendations that map to the escalation paths defined in the Escalation and SAR
Procedure. Recommendations must be actions available to a customer-care operator (document,
refer, restrict pending review, verify with customer through approved channels) - not legal
conclusions.
